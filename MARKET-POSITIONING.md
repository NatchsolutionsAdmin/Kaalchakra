## 1. The one-line position

**A stylized, story-driven 3D adventure whose core verb is deciphering an ancient script across three eras — Indus Valley heritage as its setting, not its costume.** We are not competing with photoreal AAA. We are competing in the *stylized narrative-adventure* lane, where small teams routinely outperform their budgets and where a distinctive art direction beats raw graphical horsepower.

This is the lane. Do not leave it. Every honest number in this document assumes we stay in it.

**Language rule:** make it about the Indus Valley, make it in English, offer it in Hindi. The Harappan script is undeciphered in real life, so we invent the language — it's language-agnostic by design. Hindi is an *additive* localization (bonus VO track, authenticity signal), never the frame.

---

## 2. Where we sit — the competitive map

Kaalchakra sits at the intersection of three proven, commercially healthy micro-genres:

- **Language / decipherment puzzle-adventure** — the *Chants of Sennaar* / *Heaven's Vault* niche. Small, loyal, review-driven, punches above its weight.
- **Stylized 3D exploration-adventure** — the *Sable* / *Gris* / *RiME* / *Journey* lane. Art-direction-led, small teams, strong lifetime tails.
- **Culturally-rooted narrative game** — the *Raji: An Ancient Epic* / *Venba* space, where a specific heritage setting is the differentiator and the marketing hook.

No single competitor occupies all three at once. That overlap is our whitespace.

## 4. The honest market reality

- **The median Steam game earns under $5,000 lifetime.**
- Games crossing **100+ reviews** typically land in **$50k–$200k+ gross**.
- The **top ~1%** earn millions. Plan for the middle; never budget on the top.
- What separates them: **review score ~90%+ vs ~67%**, and **wishlist velocity** before launch.

The game being *good and seen* is the whole ballgame — hence the two pillars: the vertical slice (prove it's fun) and the marketing channel (get it seen).

---

## 5. Pricing

**$19.99 base** (move to $24.99 only if production values clearly justify it). ~5 hours at $24.99 invites "too short" reviews. India regional pricing is set low (well under ₹1,000), so most *revenue* comes from US/EU/China; India drives wishlists, resonance, word-of-mouth.

## 6. Revenue model

```
  $19.99   headline price
 × ~0.60   blended realized price (regional pricing + sale discounts)   → ~$12.00
 × 0.70    after Steam's 30% cut                                         → ~$8.40
 × 0.95    after ~5% refunds                                             → ~$8.00
 ≈ $8 net to the studio per lifetime unit (before any publisher share)
```

| Scenario | Lifetime units | Net to studio | Real-world analog |
|---|---|---|---|
| Underperformance | 20,000 | ~$160,000 | below Raji |
| **Modest (base plan)** | 100,000 | ~$800,000 | Chants month-1 / Raji zone |
| Strong | 300,000 | ~$2.4M | Sable / Chants lifetime |
| Breakout | 1,000,000 | ~$8M | Gris by 2020 |
| Phenomenon | 3,000,000 | ~$24M | Gris lifetime |

**Publisher variant:** publishers (Focus, Raw Fury, Devolver) typically fund and market for ~30–50% of net — halves take-home but de-risks budget and supplies marketing. Usually right for a first title. Model both.

A lean Indian production of **$150k–$500k** is cleared by the Modest scenario.

## 7. The marketing lever — S8UL

- **S8UL** is India's biggest esports-and-content org (4× Esports Content Group of the Year, 25+ creators, 250+ brand partners). Real reach.
- It's a *content and esports* org, not a publisher or funder. Its audience skews mobile/competitive. It drives **awareness and wishlist velocity**, especially in India — it does **not** replace reaching the global Steam narrative-adventure audience (Steam Next Fest, curators, cozy/atmospheric streamers) and does **not** fund development.

**Wishlist targets:** 7–10k = modest launch; **20k+ = strong; 50k+ = top-seller position.** Launch-week conversion ~10–15%; lifetime 20–40%. Evara's in-fiction "channel" can be a real content strategy.

---

## 8. Strong vs. must-invest

**Strong:** distinctive decipherment mechanic; un-served setting; stylized art direction; warm marketing channel; systems layer buildable with AI before art spend.

**Must invest:** animation polish (esp. facial); technical art/lighting; global marketing reach; QA across hardware.

---

## 9. The pitch, in one paragraph

Kaalchakra is a stylized, culturally-rooted, decipherment-driven 3D adventure aimed squarely at a lane where two-to-thirteen-person teams have repeatedly turned modest budgets into six-, seven-, and occasionally eight-figure outcomes — *Chants of Sennaar* (~$1M in month one, two people), *Sable*, *Raji* (India, funded a sequel), and *Gris* (3 million copies). We are not chasing photoreal AAA; we are executing a proven small-team playbook with a fresh mechanic, an underserved setting, and a top-tier Indian content channel already in reach. The ask is "fund the vertical slice that proves the fun, and the marketing runway that gets it wishlisted."

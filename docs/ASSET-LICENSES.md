> waiting for the day the game makes money (`CLAUDE.md` §3, Ownership).

> **Allowed:** CC0 / public domain, or a paid tier that explicitly grants
> commercial use with no revenue share and no required credit.
> **Not allowed:** anything "free for personal use", anything requiring
> attribution we are not prepared to give, anything with a revenue share.
